# LumiFX

**Stories and scenes for Philips Hue.** LumiFX turns Philips Hue lights into
living atmospheres and narrative lighting stories, with Music Sync from the
phone's microphone. Everything runs on your phone and your home network:
no account, no servers, no tracking.
