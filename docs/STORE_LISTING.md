## Google Play: Data safety form

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
  **Yes**, one, because of Weather lights (everything else stays on the
  device):
  - **Location › Approximate location: collected, not shared.** The city
    the user types, and the coordinates of the place they pick, are sent
    to Open-Meteo to get the weather. Collection is **optional** (only if
    the user opens Weather lights and searches); purpose **App
    functionality**. Answer **No** to "processed ephemerally": the
    request goes to a third-party service we do not control, so we
    cannot promise how long it keeps it. It is not "shared" in Play's
    sense: the
    transfer is initiated by the user to get exactly that weather. The
    app never requests the device's location permission.
  - If Play insists on a "shared" answer, declare it shared with purpose
    App functionality; nothing else changes.
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
  **Yes** (HTTPS to Open-Meteo).
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
  **No**: LumiFX keeps nothing on any server; the chosen place lives on
  the phone and goes with "Change" or uninstalling. (Bridge credentials
  go with "Forget this bridge".)
- Microphone: the app uses RECORD_AUDIO but does not collect audio. In the
  data types list, do **not** declare "Audio files" or "Voice or sound
  recordings"; audio is processed ephemerally on device and never stored
  or sent (Play's definition of "collected" excludes ephemeral processing).
- Photos: "From a photo" (scene editor) uses the system photo picker or
  camera; the picture is read once on the device to take its colours and
  is never stored or sent. Do **not** declare "Photos". Android needs no
  storage permission (the photo picker is used).
- Camera: the app uses CAMERA for TV Sync but does not collect images.
  Do **not** declare "Photos" or "Videos"; frames are averaged ephemerally
  on device and never stored or sent. A camera is not required to install
  (`android.hardware.camera.any` is marked not required) and the camera
  plugin's WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is removed from the manifest.
- Advertising ID: not used.

**Permissions declaration:** none of the sensitive permissions (SMS, call
log, location, all files) are requested. RECORD_AUDIO and CAMERA need no
separate declaration form.

**Foreground service declaration (App content › Foreground service
permissions):** the app declares a `mediaProjection` foreground service,
used only for "Music Sync source › This phone's audio" (Android 10+).
Answer: task type **Media projection**; description "Captures the audio the
phone is playing so the lights can follow it, while a Music Sync effect
runs. Started by the user from the player after Android's consent dialog,
with an ongoing notification; nothing is recorded or saved." Play asks for
a short video: record Settings › Music Sync source › This phone's audio,
then Interactive › Beat Pulse › Play, the Android consent dialog, the
notification and the lights following the music (see `docs/QA_CHECKLIST.md`
› Music Sync, phone audio).

The app also declares a second foreground service, `PlaybackService`, of
the types **Connected device** and **Microphone**: "Keeps controlling the
user's Philips Hue lights over the local network while an effect the user
started plays with the screen off (and listens to the room during a Music
Sync effect). Ongoing notification with a Stop button; stops when the
effect stops. Can be turned off in Settings." Video: start Ocean on a room,
lock the phone, show the lights still moving and the notification, press
Stop in the notification.

**Content rating questionnaire:** no violence, no sexual content, no
language, no controlled substances, no user interaction, no sharing of
location, no purchases. Result: Everyone (ESRB) / PEGI 3.

**Target audience:** 18 and over (simplest; the app is not designed for
children). Do not tick "designed for families".

## App Store Connect: App Privacy

- Data collection: **Coarse Location** only, because of Weather lights:
  not linked to the user's identity, not used for tracking, purpose **App
  Functionality**. (The place the user types is sent to Open-Meteo.
  Microphone audio, photos and camera frames are processed on device and
  not stored; bridge credentials never leave the device.) This matches
  `NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes` in `ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.
- Tracking: **No.**
- Privacy manifest: `ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` (no tracking, no
  collected data except coarse location for Weather lights, UserDefaults
  reason CA92.1).

**Export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false`, so
TestFlight does not ask on every build. The app uses only standard
algorithms: TLS to the bridge (OS), OS secure storage, and AES-128-GCM for
the Hue DTLS stream, implemented with the open-source `pointycastle`
library rather than taken from the OS. Standard, non-proprietary
encryption falls under mass-market category 5D992.c: exempt from Apple's
documentation requirements, but US law expects an annual self-
classification report to BIS/NSA (a short e-mail; see Apple's "Complying
with Encryption Export Regulations"). If you prefer to be asked every
time, remove the key from `ios/Runner/Info.plist` and answer in App Store
Connect: uses encryption → yes; proprietary algorithms → no; standard
algorithms → yes; not a French app.

**Mac availability:** the app is iPhone-only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`)
but App Store Connect offers it on Apple-silicon Macs by default. Untick
"Make this app available on Mac" (Pricing and Availability) unless you have
tested microphone and local-network access on a Mac.

**Age rating:** answer "None" to every question → 4+.

**Photosensitivity:** effects with lightning or beats show a one-time
warning before they first play, with a "Soften flashes" choice that is also
a switch in Settings (it limits how fast brightness may change). Mention it
in the review notes if a reviewer asks about flashing content.

> Local-network permission (iOS) is needed to discover and talk to the Hue
> Bridge. Microphone permission is requested only when a Music Sync effect
> is started; audio is analysed in real time on the device and never
> recorded or transmitted. On Android 10+, Settings offers "This phone's
> audio" as the Music Sync source: it uses Android's playback capture
> (media projection consent and a foreground-service notification) to
> receive the sound of other apps, never the screen. Camera permission is
> requested only when TV Sync (Interactive › TV Sync) is started: the
> camera looks at the TV and a few pixels of each frame are averaged on the
> device to colour the lights; nothing is recorded or transmitted.
> Siri phrases (iOS) and Google Assistant App Actions only open LumiFX's
> own links; no speech is handled by the app itself.
> Photo library access is used only when the user taps "From a photo" in
> the scene editor, to take the colours of one photo on the device. At a
> birthday party (Parties and games › Birthday party) the microphone is
> used only after tapping "Blow on the phone", to hear the candles being
> blown out; nothing is recorded. Weather lights (home › Weather lights)
> send the city the user types to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to show the
> weather; the device location is never requested.

> LumiFX maakt van Philips Hue lampen een belevenis in plaats van een
> afstandsbediening.
>
> SCÈNES
> Haardvuur, Oceaan, Noorderlicht, Zonsondergang, Onweer, Disco: levende
> sferen die blijven bewegen zolang je wilt, elk in meerdere kleurvarianten.
>
> VERHALEN
> Korte lichtverhalen die zich in de kamer afspelen: een deugniet in het bos,
> een slapende draak, een spookhuis in een stormnacht, een schattenjacht, een
> kraken onder het schip, een meteorenregen, naderende dinosaurussen, de
> diepzee bij nacht. Met Halloween: een pompoenveld, de heksenketel, het
> kasteel van de vampier en trick or treat; met Kerst: de werkplaats van de
> Kerstman, stille sneeuwval en een kerstlied bij kaarslicht. Elke ronde van
> een verhaal is net even anders.
>
> EIGEN SCÈNES EN AFSPEELLIJSTEN
> Kies twee tot vijf kleuren en een beweging voor je eigen scène. Zet
> effecten in een afspeellijst voor de hele avond, op volgorde of
> willekeurig, met zachte overgangen. Effecten blijven spelen terwijl je
> door de app bladert, met een minispeler op elk scherm. Sla het licht van
> dat moment op als Hue-scène en start effecten vanuit Siri-opdrachten of
> Android-automatiseringen met lumifx://-links.
>
> MUSIC SYNC
> Beat Pulse, Party Chase, Spectrum en Mood Glow luisteren naar de muziek in
> de kamer en laten de lampen meebewegen. Het geluid wordt op je telefoon
> geanalyseerd en nooit opgenomen.
>
> GEMAAKT VOOR HUE
> • Vindt en koppelt je Hue Bridge op je wifi, zonder account.
> • Speelt in elke kamer of zone en streamt snelle effecten naar
>   Entertainment-ruimtes (50 updates per seconde).
> • Maak en bewerk Entertainment-ruimtes in de app, met lampen op een
>   plattegrond van de kamer.
> • Pas helderheid, intensiteit en snelheid aan tijdens het afspelen.
> • Je Hue-scènes met één tik, een slaaptimer die de lampen laat vervagen en
>   uitzet, favorieten, en je lampen gaan na elk effect terug naar hoe ze
>   waren.
> • Een stand "flitsen verzachten" voor wie gevoelig is voor knipperend
>   licht.
> • Bekijk elk effect op het scherm voordat je het op echte lampen speelt.
>
> Vereist een Philips Hue Bridge (vierkant model) en Hue-kleurlampen of White
> Ambiance-lampen op hetzelfde wifinetwerk. Entertainment-ruimtes hebben
> Hue-lampen nodig die streaming ondersteunen.

## 日本語

**サブタイトル / 簡単な説明**

> Philips Hue のストーリーとシーン

**プロモーションテキスト**

> 部屋を雷雨に、幽霊屋敷に、ダンスフロアに。Philips Hue のためのオリジナル
> の光の物語。Music Sync 対応。

**説明**

> LumiFX は、Philips Hue の照明を単なるリモコンではなく「体験」に変えます。
>
> シーン
> 暖炉、海、オーロラ、夕焼け、雷雨、ディスコ。好きなだけ動き続ける生きた雰囲気
> を、さまざまな色のバリエーションで。
>
> ストーリー
> 部屋じゅうで展開する短い光の物語。隠れたいたずら妖精、眠れるドラゴン、嵐の夜
> の幽霊屋敷、宝探し、船の下のクラーケン、流星群、近づいてくる恐竜、夜の深海。
> ハロウィンにはかぼちゃ畑、魔女の大釜、吸血鬼の城、トリック・オア・トリート。
> クリスマスにはサンタの工房、静かな雪、キャンドルのキャロル。
>
> 自分だけの演出
> 2〜5色と動きを選んでオリジナルのシーンを作成。演出を並べたプレイリストで、
> 順番またはシャッフルでひと晩じゅう楽しめます。演出はアプリ内を移動しても再生
> を続け、どの画面にもミニプレーヤーが表示されます。照明の色を Hue シーンとして
> 保存したり、スリープタイマーを使ったり、lumifx:// リンクで Siri のショートカット
> や Android のオートメーションから演出を始めたりできます。
>
> MUSIC SYNC
> ビートパルス、パーティーチェイス、スペクトラム、ムードグローが部屋の音楽
> （Android ではスマートフォン自体の音声も）を聴き取り、照明を動かします。音声は
> スマートフォン上で解析され、録音されることはありません。
>
> HUE のために
> • アカウント不要。Wi-Fi 上の Hue ブリッジを見つけてペアリングします。
> • どの部屋やゾーンでも再生でき、速い演出はエンターテインメントエリアにストリー
>   ミング（毎秒 50 回更新）。
> • 点滅する光に敏感な方のための「フラッシュを和らげる」モード。
> • 演出を止めると、照明は元の状態に戻ります。
>
> Philips Hue ブリッジ（四角いモデル）と、同じ Wi-Fi ネットワーク上の Hue カラー
> またはホワイトアンビアンスの照明が必要です。
>
> LumiFX は独立したアプリであり、Signify とは関係ありません。Philips Hue は
> Signify Holding B.V. の商標です。
